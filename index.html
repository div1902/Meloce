7 answered demo questions

01How do I book a table at Bae Sik Dang?

Choose a date from today through seven days ahead and a dinner time. Select Private Room, Main Lobby, or Room 3, then select whole tables or individual seats and press “Place reservation.” The demo is saved in this browser only; no booking is sent to Bae Sik Dang.

Was this helpful?
02Can I visit without a reservation?

In our sample policy, walk-ins are welcome when tables are available. Booking ahead is recommended for busy evenings. This is a demo answer; Bae Sik Dang’s actual walk-in policy and availability still need to be confirmed.

Was this helpful?
03Can I book for a group or a celebration?

Each demo room has two tables of 6 and two tables of 4, for 20 seats in total. You can select several tables, individual seats, or a mix. One reservation covers one room and time; create a separate reservation for another room. These are preview layouts, not confirmed restaurant capacities.

Was this helpful?
04Can I change or cancel my reservation?

Open “Edit a reservation” or “Cancel a reservation” below the room layout to visit your reservation board. Choose the specific booking to edit its date, time, room, and seats, or confirm its cancellation. Edits update the original booking; cancellation releases only that booking’s seats in this browser.

Was this helpful?
05What if I’m running late?

Our demo policy uses a 15-minute grace period. If you expect to be later, contact the restaurant through its confirmed contact channel before your arrival time. The grace period here is a sample for this preview, not an established Bae Sik Dang policy.

Was this helpful?
06Do I need to pay a deposit?

No payment is collected in this website demo. Deposit amounts, refund terms, and cancellation fees for real bookings have not been supplied yet. Check the restaurant’s confirmed booking terms before making any payment.

Was this helpful?
07How far ahead can I reserve?

Choose any date from today through seven days later, inclusive, based on Singapore time. Past dinner slots are unavailable. The date limit is checked again when you place or edit a demo reservation.
